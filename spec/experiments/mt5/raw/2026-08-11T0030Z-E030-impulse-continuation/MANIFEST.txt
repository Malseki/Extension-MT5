E-MT5-030 — IMPULSE CONTINUATION OBSERVER

experiment  EXPLORATORY on EURUSD 2024-2026 (contaminated sample).
            Pure observer: no orders, no account, no costs.
            This is the 4th hypothesis; the declared budget was 3. Recorded as
            exploratory, NOT confirmatory.

question    Asked by the trader, in two parts:
              (1) when price moves X pips in Y minutes, does it CONTINUE?
              (2) how far and how long does the move run?

design      On impulse detection, a SYMMETRIC race of +/- D pips from the
            trigger price, in the direction of the impulse. Structural null is
            50%: on a driftless walk a symmetric race is a coin flip.
            Four cells declared before measuring, primary marked.

RESULT (1) — IMPULSES DO NOT CONTINUE. THEY REVERT.

  cell                          n        P(cont)    z        95% CI
  imp  5p -> race +-10p  [PRIM] 10,674   47.95%    -4.24   [47.00, 48.90]
  imp  5p -> race +-20p          3,436   49.30%    -0.82   [47.63, 50.97]
  imp 10p -> race +-20p          1,933   49.35%    -0.57   [47.13, 51.58]
  imp 10p -> race +-40p            ~900  (secondary, descriptive)

  The primary cell is significant at z = -4.24 over 10,674 races.
  CONSISTENT with E-MT5-029: the variance ratio found reversion at every
  intraday scale on the same instrument, by a completely different method.
  Two independent measurements, same conclusion: EURUSD intraday reverts.

RESULT (2) — HOW FAR AND HOW LONG  (primary cell)

  max favourable excursion   median  9.3 pips   p75 10.1   p90 10.4
  time to that maximum       median 10.3 min    p75 35.0   p90 87.2
  total duration             median 22.6 min    p75 58.2   p90 129.0

  Half of all moves travel 9.3 pips and top out in ~10 minutes.
  90% are exhausted inside 90 minutes.

ECONOMICS — the recurring wall

  Trading the reversion of ALL impulses:
     gross EV +0.410 pips  -  spread 0.4614  =  -0.051 pips   NEGATIVE

  Fifth time in this project that real structure lands just below the
  execution tax.

POST-HOC OBSERVATION — NOT A RESULT

  Splitting by direction AFTER seeing the data:
     bullish impulse  n=5,180  P(rev)=51.14%  z=-1.64  EV net -0.233 pips
     bearish impulse  n=5,494  P(rev)=52.91%  z=-4.31  EV net +0.121 pips

  The bearish-impulse rebound is the first positive net EV of the project.
  IT IS POST-HOC. The direction split was chosen after inspecting results —
  precisely the manoeuvre that produced the $10,862 balance that collapsed to
  $12 on virgin GBPUSD.

  It is NOT a finding. It is a hypothesis that requires preregistration and a
  test against the reserved GBPUSD 2011-2023 sample, which remains untouched.

VERDICT: impulses revert, significantly and consistently with E-MT5-029.
The effect is below the execution tax when taken as a whole. The directional
asymmetry is unvalidated and must not be traded on.
