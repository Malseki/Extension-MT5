E-MT5-033 — MAKER vs TAKER, A/B ON THE SAME SIGNAL

experiment  ECONOMIC A/B. Same EA, same signal, same period, same sizing.
            One input switches market entry for limit entry. Any difference
            is attributable to that alone.
range       EURUSD 2024-2026, M5, model 4, fixed $100 risk, 1 position max
prediction  RECORDED BEFORE MEASURING:
              taker EV = -0.276 pips/trade
              maker EV = +0.167 pips/trade   <- first positive prediction
            REFUTED. The maker came out WORSE than the taker.

RESULT
  mode     placed  filled  fill%   win%      PF   balance   totalR
  MAKER      2270    2186  96.3%  46.61%  0.8935     9.92   -329.5
  TAKER      2550    2550 100.0%  48.00%  0.9181    10.67   -121.5

TWO THINGS THE TEST MEASURED THAT THE THEORY MISSED

  1. ADVERSE SELECTION, now quantified: 48.00 - 46.61 = 1.39 pp.
     Fill rate was 96.3%, so almost nothing was lost to non-execution.
     The limits fill, and they fill preferentially on the bad ones: when
     price was about to revert for real, the order sat unfilled.

  2. EXECUTION COST IS LARGER THAN ESTIMATED.
       pure observer (E-MT5-031, no costs)   52.81%
       taker, real execution                 48.00%   -4.81 pp
       maker, real execution                 46.61%   -6.20 pp
     The available margin was 0.81 pp. The real drop is 5.9x that for the
     taker and 7.7x for the maker.

     Source of the gap: the observer resolved every race against the bid.
     In real execution a short closes against the ASK. That bid/ask asymmetry
     at each barrier is a cost the observer could not see and the tester does
     apply.

VERDICT
  The maker route -- the last configuration the evidence pointed at -- also
  loses, and loses harder. There is no fourth arrangement: taking liquidity,
  providing it, small stop, large stop, fractional sizing, fixed sizing, two
  instruments, fourteen years.

  The gap between what the inefficiency is worth (0.562 pips) and what it
  costs to collect (4.81 pp of win rate) cannot be closed with execution
  engineering.

NOTE ON THE PREDICTION BEING WRONG
  The prediction was wrong in the optimistic direction, which is exactly the
  direction that costs money. It was recorded before measuring, so the error
  is visible instead of hidden. That is the point of preregistration.
