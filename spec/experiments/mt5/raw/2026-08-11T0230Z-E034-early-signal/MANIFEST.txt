E-MT5-034 — EARLY SIGNAL AS ENTRY TRIGGER, BOTH DIRECTIONS

experiment  ECONOMIC A/B. Signal = 3 pips inside the FORMING M5 candle (the
            early-warning detector added to the live EA). Two runs identical
            except for entry direction.
range       EURUSD 2024-2026, M5, model 4, market entry, fixed $100 risk
prediction  RECORDED BEFORE MEASURING: both negative, and "with the move"
            worse than "against it", because impulses revert. -> CONFIRMED.

RESULT
  direction              n     win%      PF   balance   totalR   spread@signal
  AGAINST (reversion)  2631   47.78%  0.9212    10.54   -127.9      0.7560
  WITH    (momentum)   1052   44.87%  0.7505    10.65   -122.1      0.7273

  Trading WITH the move is 2.91 pp worse than trading against it.

WHAT IT ESTABLISHES

  1. FOURTH independent confirmation of reversion, now from the economic side.
     If the market had momentum, "with the move" would have won. It lost more.

  2. The early warning does NOT change profitability, exactly as predicted.
     Earlier notice improves information about the present; it does not confer
     any ability to predict the future.

  3. Break-even with symmetric 10-pip stop/target is not 50% but 53.65%,
     because the 0.73 pip spread must be covered on every trade:
        pure observer          52.81%
        real execution         47.78%   (-5.03 pp)
        break-even needed      53.65%

ON THE SELLING QUESTION (raised by the trader)
  Selling was never under-represented. FX pairs are a ratio between two
  currencies, so long and short are symmetric -- there is no equity-style
  bullish bias. E-MT5-031 measured 62,208 bullish against 61,753 bearish
  impulses and E-MT5-023 ran 1,520 BUY against 1,656 SELL. The only asymmetry
  ever found did not survive virgin data.

VERDICT: no configuration of this signal is profitable. The early warning is a
usability improvement, not an economic one.
