E-MT5-029 — VARIANCE RATIO / STRUCTURE OBSERVER

experiment   EXPLORATORY on EURUSD (contaminated sample, already examined).
             Pure observer: no orders, no account, no costs.
question     Is there ANY exploitable linear structure in the returns, at any
             intraday scale? This is the general test that subsumes the three
             refuted directional hypotheses.
method       Lo-MacKinlay variance ratio, heteroskedasticity-robust z.
             VR = Var(r_q)/(q*Var(r_1)).  VR<1 reversion, VR>1 momentum.
range        2024.01.01 -> 2026.08.08, EURUSD

ESTIMATOR VALIDATION
  Two bugs were found and fixed before any conclusion was drawn:
    (1) overlap normalisation used (1 - 1/q) instead of (1 - q/n),
        which inflated VR to ~2 at q=2.
    (2) delta_j denominator used [sum]^2/n instead of [sum]^2,
        which inflated theta by n and crushed z by sqrt(n).
  After the fix the estimator was validated on a synthetic pure random walk:
    q=2  VR=0.99824 z=-0.78 | q=4 VR=0.99338 z=-1.58
    q=8  VR=0.98987 z=-1.53 | q=16 VR=0.98645 z=-1.38   -> all non-significant.

RESULT — structure EXISTS
  base M1, n=371,354 returns:
    ALL       VR 0.986 -> 0.897   z -2.797 -> -3.962   REVERSION at every scale
    VOL_BAJA  VR 0.966 -> 0.832   z -7.810 -> -9.937   strongest
    VOL_ALTA  VR 0.992 -> 0.926   z -1.382 -> -2.692   weakest

  This is the first statistically solid finding of the project.

WHY IT IS NOT EXPLOITABLE — two independent reasons

  (a) It dissolves under aggregation, the signature of microstructure:
        base M1   reversion at all scales
        base M5   reversion up to q=16 (80 min), gone beyond
        base M15  reversion only at q=2 (30 min), gone beyond
      And it is STRONGER in LOW volatility (z=-9.9) than high (z=-1.4),
      the opposite of what the short-term-reversal literature predicts,
      but exactly how price discretisation behaves when the market is quiet.

  (b) It is smaller than the spread. With sigma = 3.0954 pips per M5 bar
      (measured) and spread = 0.4614 pips (measured in E-MT5-024):

        q=2   10 min  VR 0.97770  capturable 0.049 pips  0.11x spread
        q=4   20 min  VR 0.95341  capturable 0.146 pips  0.32x spread
        q=8   40 min  VR 0.93346  capturable 0.296 pips  0.64x spread
        q=16  80 min  VR 0.92721  capturable 0.459 pips  1.00x spread

      At best it exactly equals the spread. Equalling the spread means
      working for free, before commission and slippage.

VERDICT: REAL BUT NOT EXPLOITABLE.

The execution-tax law discovered in E-MT5-024/025, s/(3D), is what kills it.
That law is not a defect of our implementation: it is the barrier that makes
small FX inefficiencies unprofitable for anyone without institutional costs.

HYPOTHESIS BUDGET: exhausted. 1-2-3 refuted at -7 sigma; tick order flow null
(50.20%); H-FOCAL refuted on virgin GBPUSD (-2.91 pp); this general test found
real structure below the economic threshold.
