E-MT5-032 — EXECUTION COST AT THE MOMENT OF SIGNAL

experiment  OBSERVER. No orders, no account. Measures what every previous EV
            calculation in this project assumed rather than measured.
prediction  RECORDED BEFORE MEASURING: the spread at the signal instant will be
            LARGER than the period average.                        -> CONFIRMED.

RESULT

  instrument         avg spread   at signal   ratio   slip 1 tick   TOTAL COST
  EURUSD 2024-2026     0.4273p     0.8360p    1.96x     0.0020p       0.8380p
  GBPUSD 2012-2023     1.3506p     1.7601p    1.30x     0.2389p       1.9990p

  n = 22,522 signals (EURUSD) and 253,286 (GBPUSD), over 460 million ticks.

THE MECHANISM

  The spread at the instant of a signal is 1.3x to 2.0x the average. This is
  not bad luck: the signal fires when price moves 5 pips in 5 minutes, and that
  is exactly when market makers widen their quotes to protect inventory.

  The signal and its cost are CORRELATED BY CONSTRUCTION. Averaging the spread
  over all ticks -- including the millions where nothing happens -- systematically
  understates what any strategy triggered by movement will actually pay.

CONSEQUENCE — every prior EV in this project was optimistic

  edge of the reversion effect (E-MT5-031, clean) = 0.562 pips per trade

  instrument   cost assumed    EV assumed    cost REAL    EV REAL
  EURUSD          0.4614p       +0.101p       0.8380p     -0.276p
  GBPUSD          0.6986p       -0.137p       1.9990p     -1.437p

  Real cost is 1.49x (EURUSD) and 3.56x (GBPUSD) the maximum tolerable.
  To be profitable in EURUSD the effect would need p = 54.19%. Measured: 52.81%.

VERDICT

  The reversion effect is REAL and CONFIRMED (z = +18.50, n = 108,678, virgin
  data). It is NOT exploitable, and not marginally so: the true execution cost
  is 1.5 to 3.6 times the entire edge.

  This closes the economic question. The apparent near-miss in E-MT5-031 was an
  artefact of using the average spread instead of the spread that would actually
  be paid.

WHY RETAIL CANNOT TRADE THIS

  Not because the inefficiency is absent -- it is measurably there. Because the
  cost of accessing it rises precisely when it appears. The market maker's
  widening is the mechanism that transfers the edge from the taker to the maker.
