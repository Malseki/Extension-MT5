E-MT5-035 — INSTRUMENT COST/VOLATILITY SCAN   ***  INVALID — DO NOT CITE  ***

experiment  Pure observer over 11 symbols. Idea under test: the reversion edge
            scales with the instrument (edge = 0.0562 * D, D = 2*sigma_M5), so
            what decides tradability is not the spread in pips but the spread
            RELATIVE to how much the instrument moves. The idea is sound. The
            measurement is not.
run         2026-08-10 20:45 local (23:45Z), live terminal, EURUSD M5 chart.
status      NOT PREREGISTERED. Run before writing a prediction, against
            protocol. Kept only so the reasoning is not repeated.

WHY IT IS INVALID

  1. The spread was read as an INSTANTANEOUS SNAPSHOT, not an average:
     source line 71 uses SymbolInfoInteger(s, SYMBOL_SPREAD) once per symbol.
     The CSV column is nevertheless labelled "avg_spread_pips".

  2. Four symbols returned a spread of exactly 0.0000 — EURUSD, USDCHF,
     AUDUSD, NZDUSD. A zero spread is impossible. The live MetaQuotes-Demo
     feed reports ask == bid for these symbols, so the reading is empty, not
     small. Every "POSIBLE" verdict in the CSV rests on a cost read as zero.

  3. Even a correct average would have been the wrong number. E-MT5-032
     established that the spread at the instant of a signal is 1.3-2.0x its
     own average, because signals fire when price moves and market makers
     widen when price moves. This scan compares an edge against a cost the
     project had already shown to be an underestimate.

WHAT THE SEALED CSV ACTUALLY SAYS ABOUT GOLD

     XAUUSD,2,19999,53.0000,388.4685,0.1364,43.6639,no cubre

  cost 53.00 against an edge of 43.66 -> margin -9.34, verdict "no cubre".
  Any later note claiming gold showed a cost of ~30 and a margin of +13.66
  does not come from this experiment. There is no run behind that number.

WHAT REMAINS TRUE

  The ratio spread/sigma is the right quantity to compare across instruments.
  Nothing here establishes that gold does or does not clear its cost, and
  nothing here says anything about whether reversion exists in gold at all —
  the 0.0562 coefficient was measured on GBPUSD and has never been tested on
  any metal.

DECISION (trader, 2026-08-11)
  Scope restricted to EURUSD. Rationale: the methodology itself is
  instrument-specific, not just the thresholds — which extends the lesson
  already recorded in FINDINGS-001 §4.2. The gold line is not pursued.

VERDICT: measurement discarded. The question it asked stays open and would
need a real spread measurement, sampled at the instant of the signal, per
instrument.
